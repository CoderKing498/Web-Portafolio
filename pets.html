Kira, a Siberian Husky dog

The Siberian Husky is a medium-sized, working sled dog known for its thick double coat, erect ears, and high endurance. Originally bred by the Chukchi people, they are active, intelligent, and independent, with a friendly, mischievous personality.

Cuinique

It is a small ground squirrel endemic to Mexico, commonly found in Guerrero, Michoacán, and Puebla. Grayish-brown in color, it measures 18 to 50 cm (including the tail), is diurnal, hoards food, and builds burrows in soft soil in arid areas.

Morrocoya

A medium-sized terrestrial tortoise native to the forests and savannas of South America, spanning from Panama to Paraguay. It is widely recognized for its distinctive dark shell with orange or yellow markings and red-tinted legs and head.

Mixed-breed cat

The mixed-breed cat, commonly known as a “moggie” or house cat, is a cat without a defined pedigree, characterized by its great genetic diversity, high resistance to hereditary diseases, and longevity, often living for more than 15/20 years.

Albino Python

A type of snake that is born with a genetic abnormality known as albinism, which is the lack of pigmentation in the body and eyes. This is why they are the yellow and white coloration instead of the normal browns and greens.

Xoloitzcuintle

An ancient, rare hairless dog breed originating from Mexico, often referred to as the "Mexican Hairless Dog". Reaching back over 3,000 years, these dogs were revered by the Aztecs as guardians against evil spirits and guides for souls to the underworld.
