Kira, a Siberian Husky dog

The Siberian Husky is a medium-sized, working sled dog known for its thick double coat, erect ears, and high endurance. Originally bred by the Chukchi people, they are active, intelligent, and independent, with a friendly, mischievous personality.

Magnolia, a Red-footed tortoise(Morrocoya)

The Morrocoy (Chelonoidis carbonarius), also known as the red-footed tortoise or morrocoy sabanero in Colombia and Venezuela, is a medium-sized terrestrial tortoise native to the forests and savannas of South America, spanning from Panama to Paraguay. It is widely recognized for its distinctive dark shell with orange or yellow markings and red-tinted legs and head.

Cuinique

The cuinique (Notocitellus adocetus) is a small ground squirrel endemic to Mexico, commonly found in Guerrero, Michoacán, and Puebla. Grayish-brown in color, it measures 18 to 50 cm (including the tail), is diurnal, hoards food, and builds burrows in soft soil in arid areas.
